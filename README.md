# HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## About the Project

I created this HR Analytics dashboard in Power BI to understand employee attrition and the overall employee distribution of an organization.

The dashboard shows employee count, active employees, attrition, salary slabs, departments, age groups, gender, job satisfaction, and work experience.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV

## Dashboard

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR%20Analytics%20Dashboard.png)

## Main KPIs

- Total Employees: 1,417
- Active Employees: 1,186
- Attrition Count: 231
- Attrition Rate: 16.3%
- Average Age: 36.94
- Average Experience: 7.04 years

## What I Analyzed

### Employee Attrition
I analyzed employee attrition by department, salary slab, gender, job satisfaction, and total experience.

### Employee Distribution
I looked at employee distribution by age group and department to understand the workforce structure.

### Salary Analysis
The dashboard compares employees who stayed and employees who left across different salary slabs.

### Job Satisfaction
I compared attrition across job roles and different job satisfaction levels.

## Key Findings

- The overall attrition rate is 16.3%.
- 231 employees have left the organization.
- Administration has the highest attrition count.
- Operations has the highest number of employees.
- The 26–35 age group has the largest number of employees.
- Attrition is higher among employees with lower experience levels.
- Attrition can also be compared across salary and job satisfaction levels.
